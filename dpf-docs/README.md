# Deepfense Coin / DPF Documentation

Bo tai lieu nay mo ta cach nhung Deepfense Coin (DPF) vao website Deepfense nhu mot phuong tien trao doi, diem thuong hoc tap va cong nghe Web3 tuy chon cho nguoi dung nang cao.

DPF khong duoc dinh huong la memecoin, tai san dau co hay san pham dau tu. Trong giai doan hien tai, DPF la reward/utility token cho nen tang Deepfense: nguoi dung hoc, lam thu thach, vuot qua kich ban lua dao, nhan chung chi, tich luy xu ao tren web va co the dung xu do de mo khoa mot so noi dung nang cao.

## Trang thai hien tai

- Token name: Deepfense Network
- Symbol: DPF
- Total supply: 2,014,000,000 DPF
- Y nghia tong cung: nam 2014, moc gan voi su xuat hien va phat trien cua GAN
- Network hien tai: Polygon Amoy testnet
- Contract address: `0xFB5605c397257267C6E90C6224D5F4826A4A742D`
- Contract da verify tren Amoy
- Supply hien dang nam 100% o vi admin/deployer
- DPF co the transfer tren Amoy, nhung website se uu tien so du ao/off-chain truoc

## Mo hinh san pham

Deepfense se van cho nguoi dung pho thong trai nghiem cac noi dung mien phi ma khong bat buoc dung vi crypto. DPF duoc thiet ke thanh hai lop:

1. DPF web: so du ao luu trong database, dung de thuong, mo khoa khoa hoc/lab/chung chi va hien thi leaderboard.
2. DPF on-chain: ERC-20 tren Polygon Amoy, chi dung khi nguoi dung lien ket MetaMask va muon rut token ve vi.

Ti le quy doi mac dinh la `1 web DPF = 1 on-chain DPF`. Khi user rut, so du DPF web bi tru va he thong tao request chuyen DPF on-chain tu vi reward/admin sang vi cua user.

## Nguyen tac san pham

- Khong pump, khong dump, khong tao thanh khoan trong giai doan testnet.
- Khong uu tien listing DEX/CEX.
- DPF la cong cu khuyen khich hoc tap va tham gia he sinh thai Deepfense.
- Nguoi dung co the dung DPF web ma khong can biet Web3.
- Nguoi dung nang cao co the lien ket MetaMask tren Amoy de rut token.
- Admin co the cap DPF web hoac gui DPF on-chain cho user de khuyen khich trai nghiem Web3.
- Moi thay doi so du phai di qua ledger, khong cong/tru truc tiep ma khong co lich su.
- Private key admin/reward wallet khong bao gio duoc dua len frontend.
