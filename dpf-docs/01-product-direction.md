# 01. Product Direction

## DPF la gi?

DPF la token thuong va phuong tien trao doi noi bo cua he sinh thai Deepfense. No ghi nhan viec nguoi dung hoc tap, lam thu thach, vuot qua cac kich ban lua dao, nhan chung chi va dong gop du lieu hanh vi de cai thien nen tang.

Trong giai doan hien tai, DPF co hai dang song song:

- DPF web: xu ao luu trong database, danh cho da so nguoi dung.
- DPF on-chain: ERC-20 tren Polygon Amoy testnet, danh cho nguoi dung muon trai nghiem Web3.

DPF khong phai la tai san dau tu. Gia tri san pham cua DPF den tu viec no co the dung trong website Deepfense de mo khoa noi dung, nhan dien thanh tich va tao dong luc hoc tap.

## Nhom nguoi dung

### Nguoi dung pho thong

- Dang nhap bang Gmail hoac tai khoan web.
- Lam bai hoc, quiz, thu thach va kich ban mo phong lua dao.
- Nhan DPF web trong database.
- Dung DPF web de mo khoa mot so khoa hoc/lab nang cao.
- Khong bat buoc ket noi vi crypto.

### Nguoi dung Web3/nang cao

- Dang nhap web truoc, sau do lien ket MetaMask.
- Ket noi dung mang Polygon Amoy.
- Xem so du DPF web va DPF on-chain.
- Tao yeu cau rut DPF web sang DPF on-chain neu du dieu kien.
- Co the nhan DPF on-chain tu admin/campaign va dung no de chung minh quyen unlock.

### Admin/Founder

- Nam giu 100% tong cung ban dau.
- Quan ly reward pool, treasury va cac dot phan phoi.
- Cap DPF web cho user/campaign/scholarship.
- Duyet rut token trong giai doan test.
- Chuyen sang tu dong hoa withdrawal khi he thong du an toan.

## Vai tro chinh cua DPF

- Thuong cho viec vuot qua kich ban lua dao trong tab Mo phong lua dao.
- Thuong cho viec lam thu thach nho, quiz va bai tap thuc hanh.
- Thuong khi hoan thanh khoa Basic, Advanced hoac Expert.
- Mo khoa mot so noi dung Advanced, mini-lab, case study, certificate upgrade hoac tinh nang premium.
- Gan voi huy hieu Basic, Advanced, Expert tren profile.
- Lam diem xep hang tren leaderboard va bang vinh danh.
- Tao cau noi giua san pham hoc tap Web2 va trai nghiem Web3 testnet.

## Dieu gi nen mien phi?

Deepfense van can than thien voi nguoi dung binh thuong, nen khong nen khoa qua nhieu thu sau DPF.

Nen mien phi:

- Trang chu, cong cu nhan dien co ban, mot phan simulator.
- Khoa Basic hoac it nhat phan nhap mon cua Basic.
- Mot so kich ban lua dao de user trai nghiem ngay.
- Trang verify certificate.

Nen dung DPF de mo khoa:

- Khoa Advanced.
- Mini-lab nang cao.
- Case study thuc te.
- Mot so kich ban mo phong kho.
- Certificate visual upgrade hoac expert badge.

## Nguyen tac thiet ke

- DPF la dong luc hoc tap, khong phai rao can bat buoc.
- DPF web la trai nghiem mac dinh; on-chain la tuy chon nang cao.
- Moi reward phai co ly do ro rang, co ledger va co han che chong spam.
- Hoan thanh khoa hoc nen thuong DPF, nhung khong du de mo khoa Advanced ngay. User phai lam them thu thach phu.
- Admin co the cap DPF de khuyen khich nguoi dung, nhung viec cap phai co reason va lich su.
- Neu user nhan DPF on-chain tu admin, he thong nen cho phep sync/verify de dung token do mo khoa noi dung.

## Huong di da loai bo

- Khong tao memecoin tren Pump.fun.
- Khong tao thanh khoan ban dau.
- Khong dua len DEX/CEX khi he thong web chua hoan thien.
- Khong yeu cau moi nguoi dung phai co vi crypto.
- Khong chuyen len mainnet chi de hien logo token.

## Huong di nen uu tien

Giai doan dau nen giu DPF tren Polygon Amoy testnet va tap trung bien no thanh thu co the dung duoc trong web:

1. DPF web balance trong database.
2. Reward ledger cho moi hoat dong.
3. Unlock khoa/lab bang DPF web.
4. Leaderboard, huy hieu va certificate verify.
5. MetaMask Amoy cho ai muon rut token.
6. Withdrawal ban dau duyet thu cong, sau do moi tu dong hoa.
